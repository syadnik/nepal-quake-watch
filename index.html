Nepal Seismic Monitor

This map tracks live earthquakes in and around Nepal using the USGS Earthquake Catalog API, with markers sized and colored by magnitude and filters for time window and magnitude. You can click anywhere to count nearby events, switch to satellite imagery, and zoom to the two seismic signals recorded for the 26 August 2026 Langtang debris avalanche and flash flood, which is why I chose Nepal as my study area (USGS event page). I started from Esri's "GeoJSONLayer with dynamic URL" sample and changed the study area, basemap, symbols, and filters, then added a dashboard, a proximity tool, a Nepal boundary layer, the event highlights, and a basemap toggle.
